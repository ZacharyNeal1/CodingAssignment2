Deviled Eggs

Recipe source: AllRecipes: Classic Deviled Eggs

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Paprika for garnish

Equipment

  • Medium saucepan
  • Mixing bowl
  • Knife
  • Spoon or piping bag
  • Serving plate

Directions

  1. Place eggs in a saucepan and cover with water. Bring to a boil.
  2. Remove from heat and let sit for 12 minutes. Drain and cool under cold running water.
  3. Peel the eggs and cut in half lengthwise.
  4. Remove yolks and place them in a bowl; mash with a fork.
  5. Stir in mayonnaise, mustard, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  6. Spoon or pipe the yolk mixture back into the egg whites.
  7. Sprinkle with paprika before serving.
